Privacy Policy

Hermes Google Integration uses Google OAuth to obtain authorized access to Google services requested by the user.

The application may access Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Google Calendar data only for the functionality explicitly requested by the user.

User data is not sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties except when required by law.

Access tokens and credentials are used solely to perform authorized operations on behalf of the user.

Users may revoke access at any time through their Google Account security settings.
