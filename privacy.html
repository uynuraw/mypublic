About hermes-agent-home-app

hermes-agent-home-app is an application that integrates Hermes Agent with Google services such as Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Google Calendar through Google's OAuth authorization framework.

Information We Collect

The application may access information from Google services that the user explicitly authorizes through Google OAuth consent. Depending on the permissions granted, this may include Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Docs content, Google Sheets data, and Google Calendar information.

The application may also collect technical information required for authentication and operation, such as OAuth tokens and account identifiers.

How We Use Information

Information accessed through Google APIs is used solely to provide the functionality requested by the authenticated user.

The application uses Google user data only to perform actions initiated by the user, such as reading emails, accessing files, updating documents, managing spreadsheets, or interacting with calendar events.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

User data obtained through Google APIs is not sold, rented, traded, or shared with third parties.

Information may be disclosed only when required by applicable laws, regulations, legal processes, or government requests.

Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide application functionality or comply with legal obligations.

OAuth tokens and related credentials may be stored securely to maintain authorized access between sessions.

Data Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards are implemented to protect user information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

User Control and Revocation

Users may revoke the application's access to Google services at any time through their Google Account security settings.

Upon revocation, the application will no longer be able to access Google data on behalf of the user.

Google User Data

Access to Google user data is limited to the scopes explicitly granted by the user during the OAuth authorization process.

Google user data is not used for advertising purposes, profiling, or resale. The application complies with Google's API Services User Data Policy.
